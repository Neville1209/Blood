Every day, patients in Bamenda need blood for surgery, accidents, childbirth and illness.

Register as a donor

Why donate blood?

Blood cannot be made in a factory. It can only come from generous people like you. One donation can help save up to three lives. The process is safe, simple and takes less than one hour.

How it works

  • Fill in the registration form on the "Become a Donor" page.
  • Come to the donation center at the date you choose.
  • A health worker checks your health and blood pressure.
  • You donate blood and rest with a drink and a snack.
