About us

The Bamenda Blood Donation Center is a student project that connects people who want to donate blood with hospitals that need it. Hospitals in the North West Region often run short of blood, especially for emergencies, so we want to make it easier for donors to register.

Our mission

To make sure that no patient in Bamenda and the surrounding towns waits for blood because there is not enough available.

Who can donate?

  • You are between 18 and 65 years old.
  • You weigh at least 50 kg.
  • You are in good health on the day of donation.
  • You have not donated blood in the last 3 months.
Note: A health worker will make the final decision on the day. You may be asked to wait if you have a fever, are pregnant, or have recently had surgery or a tattoo.

Frequently asked questions

Does it hurt?

You will feel a small pinch when the needle goes in. After that it is not painful.

Is it safe?

Yes. A new sterile needle is used for every donor and is thrown away afterwards.

How much blood is taken?

About 450 ml. Your body replaces it within a few weeks.
